The team behind the tolerance

Mac Precitec India Pvt Ltd was founded in 2013 by engineers with three decades in the cutting tool industry. Today more than 55 people design, manufacture and prove out tools under one roof in Peenya.

Precision tooling, one roof

Mac Precitec India Pvt Ltd manufactures PCD tools, fine boring tools, ISO tools, tool holding systems and setting fixtures for the automobile, aerospace, defence, medical, power generation and machine-building sectors. Design, planning, PCD manufacturing and quality assurance all sit inside the same 15,000 sq ft facility in Peenya — a drawing becomes a proved-out tool faster here than at a shop that outsources any of those steps.

Our vision

Turnkey tooling solutions, priced competitively, backed by service — and built indigenously rather than imported. That was the brief in 2013, and it still is.

Our team

Design and manufacturing engineers with three decades of combined experience in the cutting tool industry — tool design and development, tool manufacturing, and tooling application. The tools we build are proved out by the people who designed them, not handed off between departments.

Our infrastructure

Everything is under one 15,000 sq ft roof: manufacturing, design and planning, PCD manufacturing and quality assurance. Nothing is outsourced between buildings, which keeps lead times short and changes cheap.
