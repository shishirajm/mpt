Design, manufacture, prove out

Design & planning. In-house engineers translate the drawing into a tool concept — tolerances, machine interface and expected cycle time — before anything is cut.

Manufacture. PCD and carbide tooling ground to ≤3 µm on diameter, under the same roof as design and QA.

Prove out. Cycle time optimisation and statistical parameters (Cpk) delivered with the tool, not after a support ticket.

Built to the client's drawing

Special heads, faster changeover

Off-the-shelf geometry doesn't always fit the operation. We integrate and modify a wide variety of cutting tools to a client's requirements, building special cutting heads where a complex operation calls for one. Our base clamping systems are compatible with a wide range of tooling machines, so changeover between jobs is faster on the shop floor.

Discuss a non-standard tool

Inside a multi-step tool

A single MPI tool collapses rough bore, semi-finish, chamfer and fine ream into one pass — fewer setups, fewer tolerance stack-ups, shorter cycle time. Every insert position is set and verified on our own gauging systems before the tool leaves the shop floor.

Precision tooling, one roof

Mac Precitec India Private Limited manufactures PCD tools, fine boring tools, ISO tools, tool holding systems and setting fixtures for the automobile, aerospace, defence, medical, power generation and machine-building sectors. Design, planning, PCD manufacturing and quality assurance all sit inside the same 15,000 sq ft facility in Peenya — a drawing becomes a proved-out tool faster here than at a shop that outsources any of those steps.

Our vision

Turnkey tooling solutions, priced competitively, backed by service — and built indigenously rather than imported. That was the brief in 2013, and it still is.
