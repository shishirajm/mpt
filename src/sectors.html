Machining solutions for automobile

≤ 3 µm tolerance

Cylinder heads, valve housings and cooling channels are where MPI tools earn their place — precise hole accuracies, excellent surface finish and a Cpk you can put in front of a customer audit. Our PCD reamers and multi-step fine boring tools already run at India's leading automobile and aviation manufacturers.

Machining solutions for aerospace

Multi-step, fewer setups

Aviation engineering components demand repeatability across long production runs and difficult materials. Multi-step tools reduce the number of setups — and every setup removed is a tolerance stack removed with it.

Machining solutions for defence

Built around the component

Defence work brings small batches, tight drawings and no tolerance for variation between them. We design the tool around the component, then prove it out and hand over the statistical parameters with it.

Machining solutions for medical

Vibration-free machining

Medical components are small, unforgiving and inspected hard. Fine boring tools built for vibration-free machining hold the hole accuracy and surface finish these parts are measured against.
