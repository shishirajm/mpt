Privacy Policy

What we collect through this website, why, and who it's shared with.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form on our Contact page, we collect the information you enter: your name, company, work email, phone number (if provided), the tool type you're interested in, and the details of your component and operation. We only collect what you choose to type into that form — there is no tracking of your activity on this site beyond that, unless analytics is explicitly enabled (see below).

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry — to understand what you need, and to get back to you with a tool, a cycle time, or the information you asked for. We don't use it for marketing lists or sell it to anyone.

How the enquiry form works

The form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service that relays your submission directly to our sales inbox by email. FormSubmit does not appear anywhere else on this site and is only invoked when you submit the form. Please don't send confidential drawings or IP-sensitive files through this form — email us directly instead, once we've made contact.

Analytics

As of today, no analytics or tracking of any kind is active on this site. If we do turn on analytics in the future, our default is Plausible, a cookieless analytics service that doesn't track individuals or require consent. If we ever add session-recording tools (like Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity), we will only do so behind an explicit consent prompt, and personal-data form fields are already configured to be excluded from any such recording.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies beyond what's strictly necessary to remember a cookie-consent choice, if and when analytics requiring consent is ever enabled.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and, where a project proceeds, for as long as the business relationship requires.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to sales@macprecitecindia.com.
